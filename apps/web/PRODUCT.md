# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is buyers, enthusiasts, and analysts exploring public vehicle analytics and Listings on desktop or mobile web. They want to understand used-car price trends and Listing Availability across the whole Finnish market or within a selected make, model, model year, mileage range, transmission, fuel type, or other meaningful segment. Their work starts with a market question and often continues into the underlying Listings that explain the result.

The product context covers these public end users only. Private crawler administration is an operational concern, not a product audience.

Fuel-type segmentation is a planned public capability, but it remains deferred until the public filter metadata and analytics API expose a stable fuel dimension. The current UI must not imply that this comparison is already available.

## Product Purpose

Make observed Finnish used-car market data easy to explore and understand through clear charts, comparisons, and supporting Listing evidence. Users should be able to examine price levels, price direction, and Listing Availability at market-wide, make-wide, model-wide, and precisely filtered scopes, including how those patterns change over time.

A successful visit moves a user from curious exploration to clear understanding, calm confidence, and a better-informed decision. The product should support questions ranging from broad market movement to how a specific model and model year changes in price over time or differs by mileage, transmission, or fuel type.

## Positioning

Turn observed Finnish used-car Listing data into clear, trustworthy market comparisons while keeping freshness, Sample Size, and coverage limitations visible.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, and analytical. The experience should encourage curious discovery, make complex evidence understandable, and leave users calmly confident in what the data can and cannot support.

Behavioral references are Google Trends for approachable exploration and comparison, Our World in Data for transparent context and sourcing, and Datawrapper for readable, accessible data visualization. These are interaction and communication references, not visual styles to copy.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketplace clutter and advertising, intimidating financial-terminal density, decorative SaaS-dashboard styling, and charts that hide uncertainty or require statistical expertise to interpret. The product should not resemble a marketplace clone or make incomplete observations feel more authoritative than they are.

## Design Principles

- Make complex market patterns easy to see and compare.
- Distinguish observed evidence from inference or actual transaction outcomes.
- Treat freshness, Sample Size, completeness, and source coverage as part of every answer.
- Support exploration from the whole market to precise segments without losing context.
- Connect aggregate trends to the underlying Listings that help users validate them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native semantic web practices as a lightweight accessibility baseline without claiming a formal conformance target. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, adequate touch targets, reduced-motion preferences, color-independent meaning, and screen-reader-friendly chart summaries or data tables where practical. Defer a comprehensive accessibility audit until a later dedicated pass.
